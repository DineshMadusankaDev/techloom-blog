Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TechLoom (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website. We aim to keep our practices understandable and to update this page when the site or its services change.

Information you provide

The current version of TechLoom is a static informational website. It does not currently provide user accounts, a comment system, a newsletter signup form, or a contact form. If you contact us through an external service, the information you choose to share will also be handled under that service’s own privacy policy.

Technical information and hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like many hosting services, the infrastructure used to deliver a website may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time, and requested pages in server or security logs. Please refer to GitHub’s policies for details about its services and data handling.

Cookies and local storage

The current public version of this site is not designed to require account cookies or store personal profiles in your browser. Browser settings and third-party services may behave differently. If we add analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other features that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy to explain them.

Advertising and third-party services

TechLoom may consider advertising services such as Google AdSense in the future, but this policy does not claim that advertising is currently active. If advertising is introduced, third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalise ads as allowed by their policies and applicable law. This page will be updated before such features are enabled.

External links

Our articles or pages may link to external websites. We do not control those websites and recommend reading their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Children’s privacy

TechLoom is a general-audience technology publication and is not intended to collect personal information from children through this website.

Your choices and questions

You can control cookies through your browser settings. For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact route described on our Contact page. We will review this policy as the website evolves.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our website, tools, or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
